Privacy Policy

Sorbet Hair Studio is a fictional business. This is a demo site built by Corivo Digital.

Last updated November 5, 2026

Forms

The booking and newsletter forms send what you type (such as your name, phone, email and notes) to Netlify, the company that hosts this site. Corivo Digital can read those entries and uses them only to test the forms. No appointment is booked and no newsletter is sent. Please don't enter sensitive details.

Cookies and analytics

This site doesn't set tracking cookies. We use Plausible Analytics to count visits in aggregate; Plausible doesn't use cookies, doesn't collect personal data, and doesn't track you across other sites. The cookie notice remembers that you dismissed it using your browser's local storage, which isn't sent anywhere.

Other services

Netlify hosts the site, and your browser loads fonts and photos from outside services. Those companies can receive standard request details, such as your IP address.
